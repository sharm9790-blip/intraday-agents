# Intraday debate (cloud / repo version)

Arguments (optional): symbols or `scan`; `capital=<₹>` (default 100000); `risk=<%>` (default 1).

All state lives on the git branch `claude/intraday-state`. Work on that branch.

## Steps
1. **Branch.** `git fetch origin claude/intraday-state && git checkout claude/intraday-state` (if it does not exist: `git checkout -b claude/intraday-state`). `git pull --rebase` before writing.
2. **Clock.** `TZ=Asia/Kolkata date` and the NSE holiday list `NSE_HOLIDAYS` in `watcher/watch.py`. Not a trading day → write nothing and stop. Then pick the mode from the IST time (or the trigger text):
   - 08:30–09:04 or text starts `PREMARKET` → **Premarket mode** (section below), then stop.
   - 09:05–09:16 or text starts `PREOPEN` → **Pre-open mode** (section below), then stop.
   - Text starts `RECHECK` (14:16–14:22) → **Late re-check mode**: steps 3–7 but skip the Bull's own scan: the Bull, Bear and Judge look ONLY at the symbols named in the text (from `state/recheck.json`), quickly (aim to push by 14:28; `deadline` ≤ 14:30). Always write a verdict, NO TRADE included.
   - 09:40–14:35 → the normal debate, steps 3–7.
   - Any other time → write nothing and stop.
3. **Today's state.** Read `state/plan.json` and `state/watch.json` (the 5-min watcher writes `watch.json`).
   - Live plans are the entries in `watch.json` → `live` (today only) with status `waiting`, `in_zone` or `t1_hit`. Up to **3** plans may be live at once, so a live plan does NOT stop the debate.
   - Stop (write nothing) if 3 plans are live, or `trades` (trades open right now: stocks the user still holds or has an unfilled INDmoney BUY order for — listed in `taken` — plus plans that hit the buy trigger in the last 15 min that the user may still be buying; a stock sold off frees its slot; the watcher keeps this number) + live `waiting` plans whose symbol is not in `taken` is 3 or more, or `day_trades` (today's total, sold ones included) is 6 or more. A live entry with `missed` set (triggered, never bought) or `user_sold` set does not take a slot, or `day_pnl` is −2000 or lower (daily loss stop). Plans cancelled before the buy trigger do NOT count; `go_count` in `plan.json` is just a tally of GO verdicts.
   - Tell the Bull to skip the live symbols, and tell the Judge which plans are live (symbol, sector, status) so it avoids a near-duplicate bet (same sector moving together). Each plan is sized on its own (₹1,000 risk, position ≤ ₹1,00,000; MIS margin ≈ 20%, so 3 plans fit in ₹1,00,000).
   - Collect today's rejected symbols from `state/rejected.json` (today only).
   - Read `state/recheck.json` (today only): rejected stocks that have since closed ≥ 0.2% above the Judge's "flips on" level or their day high at rejection time (the watcher checks every 5 min). These are **not** skipped — give them to the Bull as **priority candidates** with their breakout line (level, time, close), ahead of new scan names (still analyse the best up to 5 in total; if more than 5, take the strongest by move above the level and relative strength).
   - Tell the Judge about the **sector-rally exception** (judge-agent.md): banks, NBFCs, insurers and capital-market stocks count as different sub-sectors; a second stock from a live plan's sub-sector is allowed only if that sub-sector's index is up ≥ 1.0% and above its VWAP, most of it is green, and the stock passes every rule on its own; max 2 live plans per sub-sector.
   - Read `state/candidates.json` (today, `at` within 20 min): the **code scanner's** ranked list of the whole liquid cash universe (Nifty 500, EQ series, turnover ≥ ₹100 cr/day, price ≥ ₹100; F&O not required) with `pct`, `vwap_dist`, `vol_x` (volume vs usual for this time), `last30`, `atr5` (5-min ATR), `fo`, `sector`, plus the market `mode` (`trend` / `choppy` / `weak`), `nifty_pct` and `above_vwap_pct`. Also read `state/pack.json` (same freshness rule): the **data pack** with INDmoney quotes, circuit limits, 5-min indicators and option-chain stats for the top stocks and live plans, and sector breadth. Give it to the Bull, Bear and Judge as their **primary numbers**; MCP connectors are only a fallback / extra (news, FII/DII, results). If a connector is signed out, carry on with the pack — do not stop. Give the Bull the top items as its **main candidate list** (after recheck priorities, skipping live and rejected symbols) and give `mode` to the Bull and Judge. If the file is missing or stale, the Bull scans by itself as before.
4. **Learn from the record.** Run `python3 watcher/stats.py state` and paste its text into the Judge's prompt under "What recent plans did". The Judge must follow its "Lesson:" lines when setting the buy zone and targets (still net R:R ≥ 1.5).
5. **Bull → Bear → Judge.** Tell the Bull and the Judge the **no-chase rule** (stocks already up > 3% on the day are skipped unless they pulled back to VWAP and bounced; prefer stocks < 1.5% above VWAP with rising relative strength and volume) and list today's premarket picks from `state/premarket.json` with their result in `watch.json` → `pre_done`. Launch `bull-agent` (candidates from `candidates.json` first, then its own scan; skip rejected symbols EXCEPT those in `recheck.json`, which must be analysed first; use TODAY's live 5-min candles; cash stocks are fine — F&O is not required; analyse the best up to 5), then `bear-agent` with the Bull's full output, then `judge-agent` with both. Run them one after another, never in parallel.
6. **Write files** (IST time, HHMM):
   - `reports/YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM.md` — all three outputs in full + the disclaimer.
   - `state/rejected.json` — `{"date": "YYYY-MM-DD", "symbols": {"SYM": "HH:MM one-line reason"}}`, merged with today's. Every reason starts with the IST time (HH:MM) of this debate, and add the flip price as `flips on 5-min close >PRICE …` when there is one — the watcher re-checks a stock once it closes above that level (or its day high). A re-checked stock that is rejected again gets its reason overwritten with the new time, so it needs a fresh breakout to come back.
   - `state/plan.json` — always overwrite with (it holds only THIS verdict's new plan; the watcher keeps the list of live plans itself):
     ```json
     {"date": "YYYY-MM-DD", "verdict_id": "YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM", "go_count": 0,
      "plan": null}
     ```
     `verdict_id` is ALWAYS exactly `YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM` (digits only, IST time of writing). If that id is already used today, use the next minute — never add letters or suffixes (`_1335b` crashed the watcher on 16 Nov).
     or, if the Judge gave GO / GO WITH CONDITIONS (top pick only), `go_count` + 1 and
     ```json
     "plan": {"symbol": "HDFCBANK", "yahoo": "HDFCBANK.NS", "side": "long",
              "zone_low": 766.0, "zone_high": 767.5, "entry": 766.5, "max_entry": 768.0,
              "trigger_close_above": 766.0, "sl": 761.8, "t1": 777.0, "t2": 784.0, "qty": 185,
              "deadline": "10:45", "cancel_if_high_reaches": 777.0, "cancel_if_close_below": 764.0,
              "exit_if_close_below_after_entry": 763.5, "time_stop": "15:00"}
     ```
     Always add `"connectors": {"indmoney": true, "investsights": true}` — `false` for any connector whose tools were missing, needed sign-in, or failed on every call this run (the watcher sends a one-time "OFFLINE — reconnect" alert).
     Numbers only, no ₹ signs. `yahoo` = NSE symbol + `.NS` (M&M → `M&M.NS`). Also add `"name"`: the company name for news search (e.g. `"Tata Steel"`), and `"entry_type"`: `"now"` (the watcher alerts BUY NOW as soon as the price is between `zone_low` and `max_entry`) or `"dip"` (waits for a green 5-min close in the zone), as the Judge decided, and `"confidence"`: the Judge's confidence as a number 0–10 (the watcher blocks weak-day longs under 6).
   - **Code checks before the verdict text (mandatory for every GO):**
     1. `python3 watcher/news.py "<company name>"` — if `level` is `high` (results, board meeting, SEBI/raid, F&O ban… in the last 24 h or today), the trade is **NO-GO** unless the event is already over and priced in; say which headline. The cloud sandbox blocks outside websites, so it normally prints `unknown`: then use InvestSights `get_stock_news` / `get_earnings_calendar` / `get_corporate_actions` for the same check.
     2. Put `"atr5"` (from `candidates.json`, or the Bull's 5-min ATR) in the plan. `python3 watcher/risk.py state/plan.json --write` — code re-sizes `qty` (1% risk incl. ₹40 brokerage + 0.04% charges, position ≤ capital) and enforces hard limits (net R:R ≥ 1.5, levels in order, entry inside zone, deadline ≤ 14:30, SL ≥ 0.7 × `atr5` below entry). Exit code 2 = **BLOCK**: the script has set `plan` to null; write a NO TRADE verdict that says "⛔ blocked by risk check" with its reasons. On PASS use the script's `qty` (and its risk ₹ / R:R) in the verdict and the draft order — never the Judge's own qty.
   - `state/latest_verdict.txt` — the Telegram message, read on a PHONE. Follow `.claude/skills/intraday-debate/telegram-format.md` exactly: Telegram HTML (`<b>`, `<i>` only), short lines (≤ 34 characters where possible), emoji markers, blank lines between blocks, no tables, no markdown, `&` written as `&amp;` (e.g. `M&amp;M`), max 2,500 characters. For **every GO**, add the **Draft order block** from `telegram-format.md` (INDmoney search line + tap-to-copy values). It is text for the user only — never call any order tool.
7. **Commit and push**: `git add -A && git commit -m "verdict YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM" && git push origin claude/intraday-state` (on a push conflict: `git pull --rebase` then push again). The watcher sends `latest_verdict.txt` to Telegram on its next run.

## Premarket mode (~08:45, overnight list)
Nothing is traded before 09:15; this builds the watchlist the watcher triggers from 09:20.
1. **Bull** (premarket mode, see bull-agent.md): overnight cues from **`state/cues.json`** (written by code ~08:05, refreshed ~09:00: US close, Asia live, crude, USD/INR, India VIX; GIFT Nifty is not in it — read the likely gap from Asia live + US close), plus InvestSights `get_market_overview` / `get_market_digest` if available; FII/DII from yesterday, news, results today, block/bulk deals and corporate actions (`get_stock_news`, `get_earnings_calendar`, `get_corporate_actions`). Candidates come from **`state/premarket_candidates.json`** (today's `date`): code ranked the WHOLE liquid universe (~234 stocks, cash and F&O) on yesterday's daily candles — uptrend (close > EMA 20 > EMA 50), close high in the day's range, volume vs 20-day average, near or at the 20-day high, RSI ≤ 72, daily ATR ≥ 1.2% — best first by momentum `score`, with `pdh`/`pdl`/`pdc`, `h20`, `atr`, `rsi14`, `vol_x20`, `ret5`/`ret20`. The Bull checks **all of the top 15 quickly** (news, results today, corporate actions, F&O build-up) and analyses the **best 8 in full**. Only if the file is missing or not today's does the Bull search by itself.
2. **Bear** with the Bull's output: results / events today, overextension (up > 8% in 3 days, daily RSI > 75), overhead resistance, weak market cues.
3. **Judge**: a watchlist of the **top 5** by momentum that survive the Bear (fewer only when fewer survive; 0 is fine, but say why for each of the top 15 that was dropped, in one short line each). For each, a plan with `buy_above` (the breakout level, usually yesterday's high), `zone_low` = `trigger_close_above` = `buy_above`, `entry` ≈ `buy_above` + 0.1%, `max_entry` = `zone_high` ≤ `buy_above` + 0.6%, `sl` at a real level, `t1`, `t2`, `qty`, `deadline` "09:45", `cancel_if_high_reaches` (= `t1`), `cancel_if_close_below`, `exit_if_close_below_after_entry`, `time_stop` "15:00", plus `symbol`, `yahoo`, `name`, `keep`: true and a short `why`. Net R:R must be ≥ 1.5 at `entry` (code checks it there; the 09:20 breakout is re-checked at its real close). Usual levels: `buy_above` = `pdh` (or `h20` when it is within 0.5% above), `sl` = the higher of `pdl`-side structure and `buy_above` − 0.6 × daily `atr`, `t1` ≈ `buy_above` + 0.7 × daily `atr` or the next resistance, `t2` ≈ + 1.2 × daily `atr`.
4. Write `state/premarket.json`: `{"date": "YYYY-MM-DD", "stage": "overnight", "id": "YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM", "connectors": {...}, "cues": "one line", "events": ["RBI policy 10:00", ...], "stocks": [ ... ]}`. `connectors` = {"indmoney": true/false, "investsights": true/false} (false = that connector's tools failed this run; the watcher alerts the user before the open). `events` = today's market-moving events only (RBI policy, CPI/GDP data, Fed decision overnight, results of a Nifty 50 / Bank Nifty heavyweight, budget); [] if none. On an event day code requires net R:R ≥ 2.0 for every plan. Then run `python3 watcher/premarket.py state/premarket.json --write` (code sizes `qty` and sets `keep: false` + reason on a risk BLOCK; use its numbers).
5. Write `state/premarket_msg.txt` (premarket template in telegram-format.md): kept stocks only, with buy-above / SL / T1 / T2 / qty and a 3–5 word reason; say the final list follows ~09:12.
6. Save the transcript to `reports/YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM_premarket.md`, commit `premarket YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM` and push (step 7).

## Pre-open mode (~09:08)
No Bull/Bear/Judge: you check the list yourself, in under 5 minutes.
1. Read `state/premarket.json`. If it is missing or not today's, run Premarket mode first (quickly: 3 stocks max), then continue.
2. For each kept stock try to read the **pre-open indicative open price and volume** (NSE pre-open 09:00–09:08): the data pack (`state/pack.json` quotes) or INDmoney `get_indian_stocks_details` / `get_indian_stocks_ohlc`. If the price equals yesterday's close with no volume, treat it as unavailable. gap % = indicative / yesterday's close − 1.
3. Rules: gap > 4% → `keep: false`, `why` "gap X% > 4%: fade risk". Indicative price below `sl` → drop. Indicative price above `max_entry` (gapped over the level): re-level `buy_above` to the indicative open + 0.1% and `max_entry` to that + 0.5%, same SL/targets, only if net R:R at the new `max_entry` stays ≥ 1.5; otherwise drop. 1–3% gap-up with strong pre-open demand (buy qty > sell qty): keep and mark `"preopen": "strong"`. No data from any tool → keep the list as it is, `"preopen": "n/a"` (the watcher still drops gaps > 4% at 09:20 from the first candle).
4. Set `stage` "preopen" and a new `id`, run `python3 watcher/premarket.py state/premarket.json --write` again, write `state/premarket_msg.txt` (final watchlist template), commit `preopen YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM` and push.
5. From 09:20 the **watcher** (code, not you) turns a green 5-min close above `buy_above` (≤ `max_entry`, volume ≥ 1.5× usual for that candle) into a live BUY NOW plan; anything not triggered by 09:40 is dropped.

## Rules
- No agent places, modifies or cancels orders, and neither do you. Never call order, portfolio, holdings, positions, family, networth, watchlist or save_analysis tools.
- If a connector (INDmoney / InvestSights) is unavailable, continue with the data pack and the other one, and note it in the report; an outage may get one short line in `latest_verdict.txt`. Groww is not used any more: never call its tools or mention it.
- Disclaimer (end of every report and verdict): `Educational analysis from market data, not SEBI-registered investment advice. Intraday trading is high risk; you place and manage any order yourself.`
